# Global Corporate Spending and Expense Compliance Policy

## Section 1: Travel & Lodging Daily Caps
All business travel lodging is capped at $150 USD (or local currency equivalent) per night for standard domestic locations, and $250 USD per night for designated Tier-1 metropolitan cities. Any lodging booking exceeding these daily caps requires prior written authorization from the department director.

## Section 2: International Travel & Flight Class Pre-Approval
Economy class is mandatory for all domestic flights and international flights under 8 continuous flight hours. Business class booking is permitted exclusively for international flights with duration exceeding 8 continuous hours, and strictly requires advance written approval from a Vice President (VP) prior to booking.

## Section 3: Approved Vendor Policy for IT Hardware and Software
All purchases of software licenses, SaaS subscriptions, developer tooling, and IT equipment must be sourced through authorized enterprise vendors (specifically: Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, JetBrains, GitHub, and Apple). Engaging unapproved third-party vendors for software services without IT Procurement sign-off is strictly prohibited.

## Section 4: Client Entertainment and Dining Limits
Expenses for business dining and client entertainment are capped at $100 USD per attendee including taxes and gratuity. An itemized invoice detailing individual food and beverage items along with the full names and company affiliations of all attendees is mandatory. Alcohol expenses exceeding 30% of the total meal bill are non-reimbursable.

## Section 5: Per-Diem Meal Allowances and Receipt Requirements
Employees on overnight business travel are eligible for a per-diem meal allowance of up to $60 USD per day. For individual meal expenses claimed separately, any expense over $25 USD requires an original itemized merchant receipt. Non-itemized credit card charge slips are not acceptable as proof of expense.

## Section 6: Financial Approval Thresholds by Role
Expenditure authorizations are governed by the following maximum financial limits:
- Individual Employee / IC: Up to $500 USD
- Team Manager: Up to $5,000 USD
- Senior Director: Up to $25,000 USD
- Vice President (VP) / Executive: Above $25,000 USD
Splitting a single invoice or transaction across multiple claims to bypass approval thresholds is strictly forbidden.

## Section 7: Prohibition of Duplicate Claims and Resubmissions
Submitting an invoice, receipt, or transaction reference that has previously been reimbursed, processed, or claimed under another report is considered fraudulent conduct. All invoice IDs, vendor references, and amounts must be verified as unique before submission.

## Section 8: Personal Expenses on Corporate Accounts
Charging personal, non-business related goods, services, or personal travel extensions to company corporate cards is strictly prohibited. In the event of an inadvertent personal charge, the employee must notify Finance and reimburse the company within 5 business days.

## Section 9: Telecommunications and Remote Office Stipends
Monthly mobile and internet reimbursement is capped at $75 USD per month for eligible remote and hybrid employees. Home office furniture and hardware purchases over $100 USD must be routed through Central Procurement rather than submitted as expense claims.

## Section 10: Foreign Currency Conversion and Banking Fees
Expenses incurred in foreign currencies must be converted to the company's base reporting currency using the official OANDA or central bank exchange rate on the date of transaction. Currency exchange conversion fees charged by banks are reimbursable when accompanied by the bank transaction statement.
